About

Why he is admired

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was a scientist, teacher, author, and the 11th President of India. He inspired millions of students to dream big, work hard, and use knowledge to serve the country. His life is remembered for discipline, kindness, simplicity, and courage.

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."

Timeline

Important moments

1931

Born in Rameswaram

Born on October 15, 1931, in Tamil Nadu, India.

1960

Started Scientific Career

Joined India’s defense and space research work after studying aerospace engineering.

1980

Space Achievement

Played a key role in India’s SLV-III project that launched the Rohini satellite.

2002

President of India

Became the 11th President of India and was loved as the People’s President.

2015

Legacy Continues

Passed away while delivering a lecture, leaving behind an inspiring legacy.
